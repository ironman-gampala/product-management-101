## The levels in one picture

```
  PM2 / L4   →  Knows the framework and covers every step.
                Needs the interviewer to pull out decisions.

  SNR / L5   →  Drives the whole answer. Every step ends in a CALL, a REASON and a REJECT.
                The plan is consistent, measurable and has a clear first step.

  SNR+ / L6  →  Strategy decides the answer. Questions the premise, sees second-order effects,
                thinks across teams and time (launch, go-to-market, org), and raises the
                counter-argument before the interviewer does. The interviewer learns something.
```

## Rules that hold for every question type

| Rule | SNR | SNR+ |
|---|---|---|
| **Decide** | End every step with "So my ___ is ___, because ___." | Make the call specific enough that it could be wrong, and say what would change it. |
| **Reject** | Say what you didn't pick and why. | Say what would need to be true for the rejected option to win. |
| **Measure** | Use an outcome metric, plus a guardrail. | Say where the metric could mislead, and how you'd catch it. |
| **Be specific** | Use real numbers, real competitors and real users. | The answer couldn't be given for a different company without major rework. |
| **Drive** | Lead every step without prompts. | Treat the interviewer as a colleague: invite pushback and use their input. |
| **Close** | Finish with a 30-second "To summarise…". | Tie the summary back to the company's strategy. |

**Time split is a level signal.** The higher the level, the more of the interview goes on strategy and
framing before the solution. At Staff (L6), that can be about 60% of the time.

## How to use these pages

1. Before a mock, read the page for that question type, especially the **checklist**.
2. Pick a sample question from the top of the page and practise it with the matching skill.
3. After the mock, check your answer against the **red flags** and log the day in
   `my-profile/daily-practice/`.

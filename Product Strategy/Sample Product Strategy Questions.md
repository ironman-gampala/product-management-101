# Sample Product Strategy Questions

> A curated bank of real product strategy questions asked at top tech companies. Use these to practice the 6-step framework, GTM, pricing, and growth approaches covered in this folder.

---

## Strategic Analysis

Questions where you play the role of a senior leader deciding what a company should do in a competitive or shifting market.

- Imagine you're the CPO of Zoom, facing a lot of competition from Teams and others. What would you do?
- How should Google respond to the Claude desktop app?
- You're a PM for Gmail. How would you react to a competing product?
- What's the biggest threat to YouTube?
- You're a PM for Apple Maps. How would you win market share? If it already has all features, how would you market it?
- Why is Android strategic for Google?
- Choose Chrome or Android and tell me why it was built.
- How would you improve YouTube's recommendation algorithm?
- What signals would you use to improve Meta's News Feed relevance?
- You're a PM for Facebook Feed. How would you decide if you should split the page into separate tabs for friends and non-friends content?
- You own an internal AI platform that other teams build agents on. What's your strategy, and if you could only own one metric, what would it be?

---

## Go-to-Market

Questions that test whether you can bring a product to market effectively — from positioning and sequencing to channel and launch strategy.

- You have invented a memory machine. Go to market.
- How would you build any product using Gen AI?
- You're a PM at Meta. Why should Meta continue investing in Reels? What goals and metrics would you set?
- Design a product roadmap for Netflix Ads.
- What device would you build to use OpenAI models in public settings like a hotel?
- Imagine OpenAI has recently built a home pod-type device with ChatGPT for public spaces like hotels, offices, restaurants. The prototype is done and ready to launch. You're the PM for home pods — what goals would you set?
- How would you create a marketing strategy for a Google bus?
- How would you convince leadership to build a wristwatch for the elderly?
- Tell me about a product you launched successfully.

## Growth

Questions focused on identifying which levers to pull to meaningfully grow a product's users, engagement, or revenue.

- How would you 10x Duolingo?
- You're a PM for Spotify. How would you increase revenue 5x in the next five years?
- How do you 2x Amazon's cart value in 6 months?
- You're a PM at Meta. As a PM for Instagram Shops, how would you increase the number of transactions?
- You're a PM at Revolut. How would you improve our referral program over the next 3 months?
- How would you expand Capital One Shopping?
- Recommend a growth strategy for Capital One Shopping.
- As a PM for Zepto, how would you develop a strategy to increase the average order value per user?
- You're a PM for Google Search. How would you grow this product?
- Propose solutions to optimize the speed of hiring developers at Revolut.
- How would you improve our hiring conversion and time to hire from application to offer rollout?
- Grow new Airbnb users by 3x.

### Invented — 2026-09-29

- You're a PM at a HighLevel-style company (all-in-one CRM, marketing and conversations platform sold directly to merchants). We launched our own Shopify-like store with the same features, but only 1% of our existing merchant base uses it. We want merchants to run their store on our platform. How would you increase adoption? *(Product Strategy — Growth / adoption of a launched product with feature parity, direct-to-merchant SaaS; Sr PM)*

## Market Entry

Questions about whether and how a company should enter a new space, segment, or geography.

- Meta wants to build an education product. Design it.
- Should Google enter the grocery market, and how?
- Should Google enter the furniture market?
- Should Google enter into the online furniture-selling market?
- You're a PM at Netflix. Should Netflix launch a podcasting app?
- You're a PM at Spotify considering adding a feature for artists to go live. Should you invest in this?
- How would you disrupt the film industry using YouTube?
- If you have allocated budget, how would you solve the climate crisis problem?
- As a YouTube PM, how would you evaluate the suggestion to develop a tool for content creators to generate ideas with scripts automatically?

---

## Monetization & Pricing

Questions about how a product makes money, how to introduce or optimize a revenue stream, or how to think about pricing tradeoffs.

- You're a PM for Max (HBO's streaming service). Roku wants to include an HBO button on their new remote control. How much should HBO pay for this feature?
- We're considering introducing a new ads placement on our app's homepage. Should we do it?
- As a PM at Meta, how would you make users feel less guilty about using Instagram while also increasing revenue?
- You're a PM at Meta. Explain why the company created Meta Pay. How would you set goals and measure the success of this product?
- What strategic reasons might Meta have for building Meta Pay? What objectives should Meta set? What metrics would you use to measure its success?
- You're a PM for Meta's Payments Platform. How would you improve the platform and what north star metric would you use?
- What value do virtual card numbers bring to Capital One?
- You're a PM at a top-up team. How would you launch cash deposits? If one of the main KPIs is down, how would you go about addressing it?
- You're the PM of Revolut's trading platform. Profits have declined to negative for the first time. How would you investigate this issue?

## Product Roadmap & Prioritization

Questions about sequencing decisions, tradeoffs between bets, and what to build next.

- You're a PM at Google. Which product would you choose to work on?
- You're the PM for one of your favorite products. What is your strategy for the next year?
- How do you prioritize competing features?
- Design a product roadmap for Netflix Ads.
- You're a PM at Google. How would you sunset Gmail?

---

## New Technology & AI

Questions about novel or emerging technologies, often with little scaffolding — you're expected to set the scope yourself.

- How do you approach GenAI safety in consumer products?
- Design the safeguards for an AI that takes actions on the user's behalf.
- How would you improve Siri with AI?
- Imagine you had all the geo-data in the world and no privacy concerns — what would you build?
- You own an internal AI platform that other teams build agents on. What's your strategy, and if you could only own one metric, what would it be?
- Why do you like ChatGPT? Who are its users, what metrics would you use to track its success, and how would you improve it?

---

## Open-Ended & Ambiguous

Questions deliberately underspecified to see how you scope, frame, and take a position without guardrails.

- Design a product to help travelers at airports. How would you go about building it?
- Design a product that helps people find contractors.
- Tell me about a user need that is not being met by the market. How would you validate a solution for it?
- What are key skills and types of information a PM should have? What strategies would you use to understand users?
- If you have allocated budget, how would you solve the climate crisis problem?
- You're a PM at Ticketmaster. How would you identify and solve the biggest problem in the post-booking experience?

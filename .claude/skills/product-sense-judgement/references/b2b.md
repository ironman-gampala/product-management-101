# B2B product sense

Use when the product is sold to businesses (Rippling, Okta, Eightfold AI, Slack, Stripe, Revolut
Business) or when the question involves sellers, brands, admins or employees as well as consumers.

## The users step changes

In B2B the person who pays, the person who sets it up, and the person who uses it are usually
different people. Name all three before segmenting, then pick one to design for:

```
  BUYER     who pays and signs the contract       (CHRO, CIO, CFO, founder)
  ADMIN     who sets it up and runs it daily      (HR ops, IT admin, finance ops)
  END USER  who is affected by it                 (employee, recruiter, developer, candidate)
```

Say: *"In B2B the buyer, the admin and the end user are different people, so let me name all three
and pick one."* Then segment inside the pick (e.g. admins at 50-person companies vs 5,000-person
companies).

Good pick reasoning: the admin is often the right first pick because admin pain blocks rollout, and
a blocked rollout means the buyer never sees value. Say why if you pick differently.

## Pain points

Walk the lifecycle instead of a single session:
```
  EVALUATE → BUY → SET UP / INTEGRATE → ROLL OUT → DAILY USE → CHANGE (join / move / leave) → RENEW
```

## Metrics

Consumer metrics (DAU, sessions) rarely fit. Prefer:
- **Activation / time to value** (days from contract to first real use)
- **Adoption** (% of seats or modules turned on)
- **Admin hours saved** per month
- **Retention and expansion** (logo retention, net revenue retention, modules per customer)
- **Trust and risk** guardrails (security incidents, audit failures, wrong-access events, fairness)

## Two-sided products

When a product serves consumers and businesses (a compare app with brands, a marketplace with
sellers), add a principle that protects the side whose trust matters most, e.g. *"Brands buy
insight and clearly labelled spots, never ranking."* Name the tension early; interviewers look for it.

## Company context (for tailoring, not memorising)

- **Rippling:** HR, IT and finance on one employee record. Joins, moves and leaves touch many systems
  at once.
- **Okta:** workforce identity (employees signing in to work apps) and customer identity (Auth0, for
  developers). Admins, security buyers, developers, end users.
- **Eightfold AI:** AI talent platform. Recruiters, hiring managers, candidates, HR leaders; AI
  quality, fairness and trust are core.
- **Revolut:** consumer and business financial app; speed, trust, fees, compliance.

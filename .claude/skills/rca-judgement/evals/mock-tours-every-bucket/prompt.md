we're doing an rca mock interview, you're the interviewer. you asked me: 'Amazon's cart conversion rate dropped 11% over the last two weeks. What's going on?' here's my first answer:

ok so there could be a lot of reasons. maybe its seasonality, like after a big sale people buy less. or maybe a competitor like walmart ran a big sale and people went there. or maybe we released a bug in checkout. or maybe marketing stopped a campaign so less people are coming to the site. or maybe there were server issues and the site was slow. or maybe prices went up because of inflation so people are buying less. can you tell me which of these happened? did we have any release, any outage, any competitor sale?

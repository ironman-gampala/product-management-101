# Sample Product Sense Questions

> A curated bank of real product sense questions asked at top tech companies. Organized by type to help you target the specific format you need the most practice with.

---

## Design a New Product

Questions that ask you to build something from scratch, often with a brief or constraint.

- *"Design a car that can fly vertically."*
- *"Design a system to track reviews abuse on Amazon.com."*
- *"Design a product for borrowing and lending."*
- *"Design a birthday app."*
- *"Design a product to help people start exercising again."*
- *"Design a washing machine for Google."*
- *"Design Netflix for kids."*
- *"Design a product that helps people find contractors."*
- *"Design a garage door opener."*
- *"Design a time machine."*
- *"Design a fire alarm for the deaf."*
- *"Design a podcast platform."*
- *"Design a rideshare product or feature specifically for senior citizens."*
- *"Design an AI product for a ride-sharing app."*
- *"Design a LinkedIn for blue-collar workers."*
- *"Design a new feature for WhatsApp group messages."*
- *"Design a next-generation elevator."*
- *"Design a product to improve the national park experience."*
- *"Design a Meta product for space travel."*
- *"Design an AI agent for a streaming service."*
- *"Design an app for renting bicycles."*
- *"Design a product roadmap for Netflix Ads."*
- *"Design an evaluation framework for ads ranking."*
- *"Design an AI evaluation system for resumes."*
- *"Design a RAG system to help TikTok's content moderation team detect and handle misinformation."*
- *"Design a product to help travelers at airports. How would you go about building it?"*
- *"Design a product for air travelers."*
- *"Design a communication tool for children."* — Stripe
- *"Imagine you're a PM for Meta. Design a product for Volunteering. What would you build? Why?"* — Meta
- *"You're a PM at Meta. Design a product for musicians."*

## Improve an Existing Product

Questions that ask you to make a real product better — start with users and their pain points.

- *"What's your favorite product and why?"*
- *"How would you upgrade a product of your choice?"*
- *"How would you improve YouTube's recommendation algorithm?"*
- *"How would you improve a water bottle?"*
- *"How would you enhance Instagram Stories?"*
- *"How would you improve Google Maps?"*
- *"How would you improve Spotify?"*
- *"How would you improve Spotify as a podcast app?"*
- *"How would you design parking features on Google Maps?"*
- *"You're a PM for Apple Maps. How would you win market share? If it already has all features, how would you market it?"*
- *"You're redesigning Capital One's Instant Push Notifications (IPN) system, moving from multiple screens to a single view. How would you improve the user experience?"*
- *"Only 1 in 5 riders tips their driver after a ride. How would you improve this?"*
- *"Design a new feature for YouTube."*
- *"You're a PM for Google Photos — how would you build the Yearly Recap feature?"*

### Invented — 2026-09-30

- *"What features would you build to grow LinkedIn Premium subscriptions?"* — Product Sense — improve an existing product with a monetization north star (not pricing / packaging); Sr PM

## Novel Tech Framing

Questions that give you a technology with no established playbook and ask you to decide what to build. The clarifying questions section is especially important here.

- *"Design a car that can fly vertically."*
- *"Design a time machine."*
- *"You have invented a memory machine. Go to market."* — OpenAI
- *"Imagine you had all the geo-data in the world and no privacy concerns — what would you build?"*
- *"Brainstorm a product idea with the help of AI."*
- *"How would you build any product using Gen AI?"*
- *"How would you design safeguards for an AI system that can take actions on behalf of a user?"*
- *"Design a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system to help TikTok's content moderation team detect and handle misinformation."*

---

## Company- or Team-Specific

Questions explicitly scoped to a company's products, team, or platform — require deep domain knowledge.

- *"How would you launch a product in the proactivity space for Gemini?"* — Google DeepMind
- *"You're a PM for Facebook Feed. How would you decide if you should split the page into separate tabs for friends and non-friends content?"* — Meta
- *"How would you build a Facebook product for blood donation?"*
- *"Design an educational product for YouTube."*
- *"You're a PM for Facebook Travel. What would you build?"*
- *"How would you build a product for movies on Facebook?"*
- *"You're a PM at YouTube Music. How would you 10x its monthly users in the next 5 years?"*
- *"Should Uber launch an 'Uber for Kids' service? If so, how would you design it?"*
- *"You're a PM for Google Photos — how would you build the Yearly Recap feature?"*
- *"Choose Chrome or Android and tell me why it was built."*

## AI Product Design

Questions specifically about designing AI-native features, systems, or products — expect follow-ups on tokens, retrieval, safety, and tradeoffs.

- *"How would you design an AI evaluation system for resumes?"*
- *"Design an AI product for a ride-sharing app."*
- *"Design an AI agent for a streaming service."*
- *"How would you design safeguards for an AI system that can take actions on behalf of a user?"*
- *"Design a RAG system to help TikTok's content moderation team detect and handle misinformation."*
- *"Brainstorm a product idea with the help of AI."*
- *"How would you build any product using Gen AI?"*

---

## Open-Ended & Ambiguous

Questions deliberately underspecified to see how you scope, frame, and commit without guardrails.

- *"If you have allocated budget, how would you solve the climate crisis problem?"*
- *"How would you disrupt the travel industry?"*
- *"How would you build any product using Gen AI?"*
- *"Tell me about a time when you maintained product craft."*
- *"Imagine you're responsible for improving customer satisfaction in baggage claim for a major airline. What metrics would you define and why?"*

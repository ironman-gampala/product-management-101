| **Okta** | Senior PM (≈ L5) | Identity: workforce identity (employees signing in to work apps) and customer identity (Auth0, for developers) | B2B product sense, strategy, technical depth, leadership scope | SSO, MFA, passwordless, admin vs end-user vs developer personas, B2B metrics |

**The pattern:** all three are B2B enterprise SaaS, and all three are about the workforce (people, their
jobs, their access). So:
- Practise mostly **B2B** questions.
- Name the **buyer** (CHRO, CIO, CFO), the **admin** (HR or IT ops) and the **end user** (employee,
  recruiter, developer), then pick one.
- Use B2B metrics: activation, time to value, seats, retention, expansion, admin hours saved.

**Level gap:** PM2 and Senior PM are different bars. The jump from L4 to L5 is bigger than from L3 to L4
(`Intro/Understanding Leveling.md`), and the strategy part of product sense is where the level is decided.

## 7. My route

```
  0–3    CLARIFY        3–4 questions in ONE batch → pick the market → "I'll assume…"
  3–6    STRATEGY       mission → why this company → why now → NSM in one sentence (write it down)
  6–11   USERS          2 lenses (why + how often) → 4 one-liners → pick 1 + why not the runner-up
  11–16  PAIN POINTS    goal → walk the journey → 3 pains → pick 1 → problem statement with "because"
  16–24  SOLUTIONS      3 lenses (information / automation / partner-social) → 3 one-liners
                        → pick 1 → 6-step flow
  24–28  MVP + METRICS  prerequisite → v1 → what waits → NSM + 2 guardrails
  28–30  SUMMARY        "To summarise…"
```

**Transition lines** (say one within 3 seconds when a step starts):
- "Before I dive in, I'd like to ask a few quick questions."
- "Let me split users by why they use it and how often."
- "Let me walk through their journey and see where it goes wrong."
- "Let me first name three different approaches, then pick one."
- "Let me walk through how it works, from entry to result."
- "Before launch, the one thing that has to be true is…"
- "To summarise…"

**Landing lines** (end every step with one):
- Users: "So I'll focus on ___, because ___. I'm not picking ___ because ___."
- Problem: "___ struggle to ___ because ___, which leads to ___."
- North star: "If we succeed, users spend less ___ or get more ___. So my north star is ___."
- Summary: "To summarise: for ___, the real problem is ___, because ___. I'd build ___, starting with ___.
  I'd know it works if ___ improves, while watching ___. After that, ___."

## 8. Three-week plan

### Week 1: build the habits (15–20 min a day)
- **Users in 60 seconds:** any app → 2 lenses → 4 users → pick one.
- **Three ideas before detail:** any problem → 3 one-liners in 90 seconds.
- **Flow in 2 minutes:** all 6 steps out loud.
- **Outcome, not usage:** 5 products a day → "users spend less ___ or get more ___".
- **The 60-second close:** prerequisite → v1 → what waits → NSM → guardrail → summary.

### Week 2: full timed mocks (3 a week)
- 2 **B2B** product sense questions (one Rippling-style, one Eightfold-style) + 1 consumer question.
- Record yourself. Target: fewer than 5 "uh"s, zero "let me take 2 minutes".
- Goal: all 7 steps inside 30 minutes, **without prompts**.

### Week 3: level and company prep
- **Senior signal:** "What should X build?" strategy questions; lead with strategy for 3–4 minutes.
- **Okta:** SSO, MFA, passwordless, workforce vs customer identity; IT admin, developer, end user,
  security buyer; activation, time to integrate, seats, net revenue retention.
- **Eightfold AI:** measuring AI quality, bias and fairness in hiring, human-in-the-loop, hiring AI audit
  laws in the US and EU.
- **Rippling:** one employee record across HR, IT and finance; what breaks when someone joins, moves or
  leaves; admin time saved as a metric.
- **Leadership stories:** 5 STAR+ stories at team-level scope.

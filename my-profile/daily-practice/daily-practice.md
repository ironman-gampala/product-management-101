**Fixed:** #9 asking for time (Days 5–6, always as "30 seconds", at most twice). #4 mixing steps fixed again on Day 6.
**First on Day 6:** the north star read back unchanged in the close, and a one-sentence problem statement (no "because" yet).
**Done once or twice:** a real guardrail (Day 4 only), "To summarise…" (Days 4–6).
**Never done yet:** a problem statement with "because" said without prompting, and a full close with a prerequisite and "what waits".

**Root cause (one line):** under time pressure I search for an *answer* instead of following a *route*. The
ideas are there; the habits aren't automatic yet. Day 4 showed how it looks in the room: **I explain before I
decide**, so the decision comes late, comes vague, or the interviewer has to pull it out of me. Day 5 added:
**I have the pieces but don't assemble them.** The problem statement came in three parts over three turns, and
the prerequisites were said as "assumptions" and never brought back in the close. Day 6 showed the same thing
between steps: **my problem statement and my solution were built separately**, so I picked a solution for a
different cause than the one on the board.

## Next mock: watch for

1. **Pick the solution that kills the "because" on the board.** Read the because out loud before picking.
2. **A real guardrail:** what gets worse while the north star rises: the other side, quality or accuracy, or trust. Not adoption flipped.
3. **Problem statement with "because", without being asked.** End the pains step with it.
4. **State, don't ask.** "I'll build on X", not "Can I assume X?".
5. **The full close:** prerequisite → v1 → what waits → board north star → guardrail → "To summarise…".

*Keep doing:* instant start with "I'll assume…"; users as one-liners; the north star unchanged to the close.

## Summary drill — pending

**The formula (20–30 seconds, no new ideas):**

```
  "To summarise:
   1. USER + PROBLEM   → for [user], the real problem is [problem], because [root cause].
   2. SOLUTION         → I'd build [solution], starting with [v1].
   3. MEASURE          → I'd know it works if [north star] improves, while watching [guardrail].
   4. NEXT             → after that, [what comes next]."
```

**Practice:** say it for [Day 1 — Google Maps parking](day-01-2026-11-14-google-maps-parking.md).
- User: the leisure visitor going somewhere unfamiliar
- Problem: circling, because they can't see where parking is or whether it's free
- Solution: find parking with availability labels; v1 = the 50 hardest areas in each of the top 5 cities
- North star: share of trips to hard-to-park areas where the user parks within 3 minutes
- Guardrail: total door-to-door time
- Next: booking
